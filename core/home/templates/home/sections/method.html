How I work with teams

Start with the decision.

If a dashboard updated every five minutes instead of every hour, what would someone do differently? I like to ask that kind of question before a team spends money changing a system.

  1. 01Diagnose

    Sit with the person doing the work. Find the delay, mistake, or expense they would change.

  2. 02Blueprint

    Compare the cost of a change with the cost of keeping things as they are.

  3. 03Prove

    Try one complete workflow. Agree on the result that would justify doing more.

  4. 04Scale

    Expand the parts that worked. Identify who reviews exceptions and pays the running costs.

  5. 05Operate

    Give the people running it the checks and instructions they need on an ordinary Tuesday.
