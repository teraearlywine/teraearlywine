How I work

A practical path to production.

Start with a defined decision and agree on scope, responsibilities, and acceptance criteria. Each stage produces the evidence and operating context needed for the next.

  1. 01Diagnose

    Find the failure modes, hidden dependencies, and actual business cost.

  2. 02Blueprint

    Choose the smallest architecture and operating model that resolves the risk.

  3. 03Prove

    Ship a production-shaped slice and measure it against reality.

  4. 04Scale

    Extend the pattern with clear controls, ownership, and adoption.

  5. 05Operate

    Leave behind a system your team can understand, run, and improve.

Support beyond launch.

Ongoing data and AI reliability support can cover incident follow-through, runbooks, control evidence, and a prioritized improvement backlog. Scope and service expectations are agreed for each engagement.
