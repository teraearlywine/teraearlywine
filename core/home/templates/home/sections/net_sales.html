Business rule

$600 − $40 = $560

Both sales are complete. The posted refund reduces net sales by $40.

Engineering note

Use the refund’s posting date and count each refund ID once. Both sales and the optional refund post in the same reporting period here. Reconcile the result to the ledger for that period.
