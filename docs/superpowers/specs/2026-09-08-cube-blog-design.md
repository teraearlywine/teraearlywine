# Growing cube blog prototype

Approved direction: the combined mockup at `/Users/teraearlywine/.codex/generated_images/01a08373-c369-7352-aea9-02feb295a65d/exec-cbdac3b8-87d0-4bdd-b5fc-74c1ad352f5b.png`.

The existing Flask site gains a local `/blog/` prototype, with Blog before Expertise. Preserve Inter, graphite `#3b3a38`, ivory `#eee8e0`, stone `#c7b7a4`, and the site's shared navigation/footer. The hero has a centered title, a real 3D glass cube, a stable article preview, and readable writing below.

Each article is one point. The first nine fill a square; the tenth starts the next layer; 27 complete a cube. Further additions occupy an expanding cube shell without changing existing coordinates. A small growth demonstration lets Tera inspect early stages. Twenty-seven explicitly illustrative local articles exercise all destinations, with no production publication or sitemap entries.

The cube is functional geometry rendered using Three.js physical glass materials and a generated studio lighting environment. It is not a static hero illustration: native buttons track its points, mouse movement gently tilts it, and selecting a point opens its layer. Keyboard focus and touch select the same previews. Rotation pauses during selection, previews remain reachable, Escape resets, and reduced-motion disables transitions. The article list remains available without WebGL/JavaScript.

Scope is a local prototype within the existing app. No new CMS, external delivery, deployment, or publishing. Blog routes are available only in debug/test contexts until separately prepared for production.

Verification includes growth coordinate tests, Flask route/sitemap/production visibility tests, desktop/mobile browser interaction checks, console inspection, and a same-state comparison to the approved visual.
